This guide explores the transition from physical to digital note-taking and provides recommendations for selecting the 
most effective tool for your personal workflow. The focus is on finding a sustainable system that matches your individual 
style rather than following popular trends.

While i love physical books, papers, and sticky notes, working with a lot of information requires digital solutions for
 us to retrieve data and review. The key is finding a notes app that's not just functional, but sustainable for long-term use.

## Here are a list of notes taking apps that I can recommend:

**My favorites:**

1. **[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)** - A powerful, flexible tool that can be customized from minimal to complex setups. 
Free, open-source, and uses plain-text Markdown files.
2. **[Roam Research](https://roamresearch.com/)** - Designed for interconnected thought with powerful linking capabilities. 
Excellent for building knowledge graphs and exploring connections between ideas.
3. **[QOwnNotes](https://www.qownnotes.org/)** - Another flexible, open-source option that pairs well with Obsidian's 
philosophy of customizable note-taking.

**Graph-Based and Interconnected Systems:**

- **[Tiddlyroam](https://tiddlyroam.org/)** - Free, open-source alternative to Roam Research
- **[TiddlyWiki](https://tiddlywiki.com/)** - No-code personal wiki system with extensive customization options
    - **[Stroll](https://giffmex.org/stroll/stroll.html)** - TiddlyWiki plugin adding bi-directional links and Roam-like features
    - **[TiddlyMap](http://tiddlymap.org/)** - Mind-mapping plugin for visual organization
- **[Dendron](https://www.dendron.so/)** - Combines Roam-like linking with hierarchical organization
- **[Logseq](https://logseq.com/)** - Block-based note-taking with powerful linking features
- **[Capacities](https://capacities.io/)** - Modern take on interconnected note-taking

**Focused and Specialized Tools:**

- **[Typora](https://typora.io/)** - Clean, distraction-free Markdown editor with live preview
- **[Hypothesis](https://web.hypothes.is/)** - Web annotation tool for social commentary and research
- **[Notesnook](https://notesnook.com/)** - Privacy-focused, end-to-end encrypted alternative to Evernote

**Other:**

- **[MkDocs](https://www.mkdocs.org/)** with **[mkdocs-newsletter](https://lyz-code.github.io/mkdocs-newsletter/)** - Documentation 
platform with change tracking capabilities


Whether you're currently using Google Keep, Notion, or any other tool not mentioned here, the principles in this guide can help optimize 
your note-taking process. Focus on sustainability, personal comfort, and the tool's ability to support your unique thinking patterns.

_Note: If you are a programmer, you can also explore templates on GitHub for personal wikis, digital gardens or even build your own website 
that acts like a personal wiki for your notes, you'll be able to customize it to your preferences._
