This is the easiest way to start, with the easiest way of working around notes effectively, if you follow this process you could have great notes.
I've personally been using Obsidian for taking notes & I've loved using that, I'd encourage you to use it as well and I understand that you may have a note-taking tool already, or that you like to write things on paper & the main point about this guide is that you could be using obsidian, vim, google notes, notion etc. and that is fine, you will need to explore other tools as well to figure out which one works best for you. 
But this guide will be useful regardless, it can even help augment your own note-taking process.
Throughout this guide I will be using Obsidian as my notes taking tool and I invite you to follow me through this journey
Taking notes effectively is a non-linear process for me & that can be quickly overwhelming and anyone could lose track of everything when you're learning, doing research or just documenting something important for you, that's why you will need to reflect on the perfect note taking tool for you & I'd advise you to checkout the following list of tools and explore the ones that feel more comfortable using
